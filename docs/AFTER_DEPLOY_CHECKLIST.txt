  3. KNOWN ISSUE — UPLOADED IMAGES DISAPPEAR ON REDEPLOY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Render's free tier has an ephemeral filesystem. Product images uploaded
  through the admin panel are saved to /uploads on the server and DISAPPEAR
  on every redeploy or server restart.

  NOT affected:
    - The 72 seeded demo products (external image URLs, not uploads).

  IS affected:
    - Any product image you upload manually through the admin panel.

  For resume/demo purposes: use the seeded products — they look fine and are
  unaffected. To fix properly, migrate uploads to Cloudinary (see prompt.txt
  task menu for the exact task description).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  4. RENDER FREE TIER — COLD START (~30-60 seconds)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Render spins down after 15 minutes of inactivity. Before a demo:
    1. Open https://<your-backend>.onrender.com/api/health in a browser tab.
    2. Wait for it to return {"success":true,...}.
    3. Then open the frontend — it will be fast.

  Optional: set up a free UptimeRobot monitor to ping /api/health every
  14 minutes to keep the backend warm during your interview.
